The World's Top Three Soccer Players

Soccer is the most popular sport on the planet, and every generation produces a few players who define the game. Here are three stars whose skill, records and trophies place them among the very best of their era.

Lionel Messi

Lionel Messi was born in 1987 in Rosario, Argentina, and is widely regarded as one of the greatest players of all time. He joined the FC Barcelona youth academy as a teenager and spent most of his career at the club, winning ten La Liga titles and four UEFA Champions League trophies. Messi is famous for his low centre of gravity, incredible close control and dribbling, which allowed him to slip past several defenders in tight spaces. He is just as skilled at creating goals for his teammates as he is at scoring them himself.

Later in his career he played for Paris Saint-Germain before moving to Inter Miami in Major League Soccer. With Argentina, he won the Copa América and an Olympic gold medal, and in 2022 he finally lifted the FIFA World Cup after a thrilling final against France. He has won a record eight Ballon d'Or awards. You can read more on his Wikipedia page.

Cristiano Ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo was born in 1985 in Funchal on the island of Madeira, Portugal. He began his professional career at Sporting CP before moving to Manchester United in 2003, where he developed into a world-class winger and won the Premier League and the Champions League. In 2009 he joined Real Madrid, where he became the club's all-time leading scorer and won four more Champions League titles. His time there was defined by his athleticism, powerful shooting and remarkable heading ability.

Ronaldo later played for Juventus, returned briefly to Manchester United, and then moved to Al Nassr in Saudi Arabia. He captained Portugal to victory at UEFA Euro 2016 and is the all-time leading goalscorer in men's international football. He has won five Ballon d'Or awards and is known for his incredible fitness and work ethic. Learn more on his Wikipedia page.

Kylian Mbappé

Kylian Mbappé was born in 1998 in Paris, France, and grew up in the suburb of Bondy. He rose through the ranks at AS Monaco, where his blistering pace and composure in front of goal quickly attracted attention across Europe. In 2017 he moved to Paris Saint-Germain, winning multiple Ligue 1 titles and becoming the club's all-time top scorer. In 2024 he joined Real Madrid, one of the biggest clubs in the world.

Mbappé became a global superstar at the 2018 FIFA World Cup, where he helped France win the tournament as a teenager. Four years later he scored a hat-trick in the final against Argentina and won the Golden Boot as the tournament's top scorer. Still young, he is expected to break many more records in the years ahead. Read more on his Wikipedia page.
